On any given day, more than one in five adults in the world — 23% — spend much of it feeling lonely.We know this from the Gallup World Poll, which in 2025 asked around 1,000 people aged 15 and older in each of about 140 countries: “Did you experience loneliness during a lot of the day yesterday?” Each country's sample is designed to be nationally representative, and together these countries are home to 95% of the world’s adults.The chart shows that loneliness (on the vertical axis) is more common in poorer countries. In countries where GDP per capita (on the horizontal axis) is below $5,000, on average 28% of adults felt lonely for much of the previous day. Where it is above $40,000, the average was 16%.Gallup reports that the same is true within countries: people who say that they find it difficult to get by on their income are more than twice as likely to feel lonely as those who say they live comfortably on their present income (32% vs. 15%).Read more about loneliness and social connections on our topic page →
