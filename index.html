DESTINY model tracks molecular chemistry on uneven, ice-covered grains in interstellar space

Interstellar space is the scene of active chemical processes: molecules form there in the gas, but also on the surface of tiny dust grains covered in ice. The paper is published in the journal Astronomy & Astrophysics.

On any given day, more than one in five adults in the world — 23% — spend much of it feeling lonely.We know this from the Gallup World Poll, which in 2025 asked around 1,000 people aged 15 and older in each of about 140 countries: “Did you experience loneliness during a lot of the day yesterday?” Each country's sample is designed to be nationally representative, and together these countries are home to 95% of the world’s adults.The chart shows that loneliness (on the vertical axis) is more common in poorer countries. In countries where GDP per capita (on the horizontal axis) is below $5,000, on average 28% of adults felt lonely for much of the previous day.
