Since 2000, nuclear and renewables have followed very different trajectories. Back then, both accounted for a similar share of global electricity. But in 2025, renewables made up more than three times as much: 34% compared to 9%.The total amount of electricity produced by nuclear plants is about the same as it was two decades ago.
